"The things you own end up owning you."

Welcome, valued customer.

SAME is a short first-person story about a woman, a television and a city that keeps telling her who to be.

Every night an ad plays on the TV. Every morning you take your wallet, walk down the street and buy the thing you saw. You come home, try it on in front of the mirror and go to sleep. The next day there is a new ad.

The street changes a little each day. The billboards change. The people change. So do you.

What you will do

  • Live through five days of the same routine: watch, buy, wear, pose, sleep.
  • Walk a crowded avenue inspired by Istanbul's İstiklal Street, full of shops, cafés, street musicians and billboards that follow you.
  • Shop in a department store, carry what you want to the till and pay for it.
  • Open your wardrobe, try on what you bought and look at yourself in the mirror.
  • Watch the world slowly lose its colour, everything except the ads and the things you buy.

No dialogue, no quest markers, no instructions. The house, the street and the TV tell the story.

Controls

  • WASD move, Mouse look
  • F interact (doors, wardrobe, mirror, checkout, lights)
  • E sit, lie down, pick up a product
  • G put back / drop
  • Esc pause menu

Keys can be rebound in Settings. English and Turkish are both supported.

Specs

Windows · made with Unity 6 (URP). A dedicated graphics card is recommended; the Graphics Quality setting has Low, Medium and High presets.

SAME, bir kadının, bir televizyonun ve ona kim olması gerektiğini söyleyip duran bir şehrin kısa, birinci şahıs hikâyesi.

Her gece televizyonda bir reklam döner. Her sabah cüzdanını alıp sokağa çıkar, gördüğün şeyi satın alırsın. Eve döner, aynanın karşısında giyer, uyursun. Ertesi gün yeni bir reklam vardır.

Sokak her gün biraz değişir. Panolar değişir, insanlar değişir. Sen de.

Diyalog yok, görev işareti yok, talimat yok. Hikâyeyi ev, sokak ve televizyon anlatır.
